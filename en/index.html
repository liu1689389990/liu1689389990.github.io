About this site

ABOUT

Daily Crypto & Web3 News is a crypto news desk updated every day. Not an aggregator: we pull the day’s primary sources (coindesk.com、cointelegraph.com、decrypt.co、theblock.co and others), rewrite each story in plain English, and add a "why it matters for regular people" note before publishing.

This site is the web edition of our Telegram channel. The channel pushes faster and reads better on mobile; the site is searchable, indexable, and every issue has its own URL.

What we don’t do: no shilling, no trading signals, no price predictions, no paid ad slots, no token or exchange referrals. Every story links its primary source so you can verify it yourself.

Cadence: three issues daily at 09:00 (overnight recap), 13:30 (midday move) and 20:00 (US close), Beijing time. One channel per language.
