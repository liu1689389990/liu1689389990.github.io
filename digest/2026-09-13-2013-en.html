Sep 13, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin price today: BTC traded near $76,700 on Sunday, Se

Bitcoin price today: BTC traded near $76,700 on Sunday, Sept. 13, down about 1% over 24 hours, while ethereum slipped roughly 2% to $2,480 and most altcoins followed lower. Tonight's crypto news: record political money and institutional treasury bets.

01

Two crypto billionaires give Reform UK a record $97M in 24 hours

Crypto investor Christopher Harborne donated £36 million ($48.7 million) to Nigel Farage's Reform UK party on Saturday, matching the gift BitMEX co-founder Ben Delo made a day earlier. The two donations total £72 million ($97.4 million), the largest individual political gifts in U.K. history, The Guardian reported.

Harborne, an early investor in Tether and crypto exchange Bitfinex, said he expects nothing in return. The Metropolitan Police are already probing earlier Reform UK donations, and the U.K. government has floated a ban on crypto-funded political gifts.

Why it matters

Crypto wealth is entering electoral politics directly, and regulators are likely to respond with tighter donation rules.

02

Ripple targets a $13 trillion corporate treasury market with RLUSD

Ripple sees company treasuries as the next growth engine for its RLUSD stablecoin, aiming at corporate clients that process roughly $13 trillion in transactions a year, stablecoin chief Jack McDonald told CoinDesk.

RLUSD circulation jumped more than 50% in a month to $2.4 billion, and daily activity has more than tripled since January to about $750 million. Ripple also plans a MiCA-compliant dual-issuance structure to bring RLUSD to Europe.

Why it matters

Stablecoins are moving from trading tools to corporate cash management, a larger and stickier source of demand.

03

Bitcoin Suisse makes the case for swapping bonds for bitcoin

The largest U.S. cloud giants will spend upwards of $800 billion on AI this year and more than $1 trillion in 2027, according to Bitcoin Suisse's Crypto Wealth Management Report 2026. The firm argues that this concentration, plus rising government debt, is weakening bonds' traditional role as a portfolio buffer.

Its modeling found that adding bitcoin at allocations from 1% to 10% improved both absolute and risk-adjusted historical returns. The report also expects ethereum to benefit as AI agents increasingly use programmable blockchain finance.

Looking ahead: the Fed rate decision on Sept. 16 is the week's main event for crypto markets. Until then bitcoin, ethereum and the wider altcoin market stay rangebound.

Why it matters

The pitch is aimed at conservative wealth managers, the investors least likely to hold crypto today.
