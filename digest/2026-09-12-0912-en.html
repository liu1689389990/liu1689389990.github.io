Sep 12, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin price today (Sept. 12): BTC rebounded past $79,000

Bitcoin price today (Sept. 12): BTC rebounded past $79,000 after US CPI data met forecasts, with Fed rate hike odds near 85% ahead of the Sept. 16 meeting. Plus: the EU flags prediction markets, and Bitwise closes its Dogecoin ETF.

01

Bitcoin rebounds past $79,000 as CPI meets forecasts and rate-hike odds hit 85%

Bitcoin jumped more than 3% and briefly traded above $79,000 on Thursday after the August US CPI report came in at 3.4% year over year, broadly matching expectations. It had first dipped to $76,000. Core CPI rose 0.3% from July, above the 0.2% forecast.

Futures markets now price in an 85% chance of a Fed rate hike at the Sept. 16 meeting, Cointelegraph reported. The 30-year US Treasury yield hit its highest level since June 2004 before easing to 5.309%.

Why it matters

QCP analysis warned rising bond yields will cause Bitcoin pain if the Fed tightens. Trading firm The Kobeissi Letter called it "a nervous market."

02

EU regulator says prediction markets are 'rife with inside trading'

ESMA, the EU's securities watchdog, devoted a chapter of its latest risk monitor to prediction markets, citing insider trading, manipulation and retail harm on platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket.

The report listed linked wallets that made $2.4 million betting on the strike on Iran with a 98% win rate, and a US Army master sergeant charged over $400,000 of Polymarket profits. A Wall Street Journal finding cited by ESMA showed 67% of Polymarket gains went to 0.1% of accounts.

Why it matters

The two platforms traded a combined $44.8 billion in June, according to The Block, and formal EU scrutiny raises the chances of stricter rules in Europe.

03

Bitwise to close its Dogecoin ETF after less than a year

Bitwise will liquidate its Dogecoin ETF, BWOW, with Oct. 14 expected as its final trading day on NYSE Arca and cash payments to shareholders on Oct. 22.

The fund launched Nov. 26, 2025 and held about $722,000 in net assets as of Sept. 8. Bitwise said the decision was part of an effort to "optimize its product range."

The week ahead centers on the Fed's Sept. 16 meeting. With hike odds near 85% and bond yields at multiyear highs, expect choppy prices across crypto.

Why it matters

With under $1 million in assets, BWOW shows meme-coin ETFs still struggle to draw lasting demand from brokerage customers.
