Sep 15, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin price today: BTC trades near $77,900 on Sept. 15 a

Bitcoin price today: BTC trades near $77,900 on Sept. 15 after briefly topping $79,000, as Trump's Iran comments cooled oil and traders put Fed rate hike odds above 90%. Here's the crypto news moving markets.

01

Bitcoin tops $79,000 as Trump hints the Iran war could end

Bitcoin rose above $79,000 after Monday's Wall Street open, up around 3% on the day, before easing to trade near $77,900. President Trump wrote on Truth Social that Iran "wants to make a deal, quickly and badly," a signal that pushed oil prices lower.

Futures markets now price more than a 90% chance of a 25-basis-point Fed rate hike on Wednesday, according to Cointelegraph. Bitcoin is also testing its 50-week exponential moving average after closing below that line on Sunday.

Why it matters

Falling oil eases inflation pressure, but a near-certain rate hike still caps upside for bitcoin and altcoins including Solana and XRP.

02

Senate Republicans make a 'final' Clarity Act offer ahead of Tuesday's vote

Senate Republicans released a revised Clarity Act late Sunday, calling it their "last, best and final offer" before Tuesday's procedural vote. President Trump agreed to tougher ethics rules, including divestment or blind trusts and enforcement by state attorneys general, Decrypt reported.

Invoking cloture needs 60 votes, so Republicans need at least seven Democrats to cross over. Galaxy Digital's Alex Thorn raised his odds of the crypto bill passing in 2026 from 10% to 25%.

Why it matters

The Clarity Act would write the rulebook for crypto regulation in the US, and Tuesday's result could move markets before the Fed decision on Wednesday.

#ClarityAct#Regulation#CryptoNews
03

Bitmine nears 6 million ETH after a $68M weekly buy

Bitmine Immersion Technologies bought 27,180 ETH last week, taking holdings to 5,956,378 ETH worth about $14.89 billion, or roughly 4.9% of Ethereum's supply. Chairman Tom Lee said the company has bought ethereum every week since it started its treasury strategy on June 30, 2025.

About 85% of its ETH is staked to help secure the blockchain, generating projected annualized revenue of $334 million at a 2.62% yield. Lee said ETH is the best-performing macro asset of the quarter, ahead of the S&P 500 by 5,866 basis points.

Why it matters

With one firm now holding nearly 5% of all ETH, treasury buyers are becoming a force that can move ethereum prices on their own.

This week decides a lot: the Senate votes on the Clarity Act on Tuesday and the Fed announces its rate decision on Wednesday. Expect choppy markets in crypto until both land.
