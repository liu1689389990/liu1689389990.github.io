Sep 14, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin price today: BTC trades near $77,800, up 1.4% in 2

Bitcoin price today: BTC trades near $77,800, up 1.4% in 24 hours, as crypto sits out an AI-driven stock selloff on Sept. 14, 2026. Bitcoin ETF flows turned negative last week, and the Fed rate decision lands Wednesday. Here's today's crypto news.

01

Bitcoin climbs to $78,000 as crypto sits out the AI selloff

Bitcoin rose 1.9% from midnight UTC to touch $78,280, according to CoinDesk data, while Nasdaq 100 futures fell 1.65% after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called for slower AI development. South Korea's Kospi index slid 3.26%.

The breadth was strong: 94 of the 100 CoinDesk 100 constituents traded higher, with altcoins including Solana up 1.6% and XRP up 4.1%. Crude oil gained almost 4% after a Saudi pipeline closure.

Bitcoin remains 4.8% below this month's high of $82,284, consolidating the run from $64,000 in August.

Why it matters

Bitcoin moved opposite tech stocks today. If the AI selloff deepens, traders will watch whether crypto keeps its bid or follows equities lower.

02

Bitcoin ETFs shed $463M last week as Ethereum funds gain $197M

US spot bitcoin ETFs posted $462.7 million in net outflows last week, ending three straight weeks of inflows, according to Farside Investors. Ethereum ETFs went the other way, drawing nearly $197 million in net inflows.

Thursday's $282.7 million withdrawal was the largest daily outflow since July. ARK 21Shares led the week with $234.2 million out, followed by Grayscale's GBTC at $129.1 million. BlackRock's ETHA pulled in $148.8 million on Friday alone.

For September, bitcoin ETFs are still net positive at about $307.3 million.

Why it matters

The split suggests large investors rotated from bitcoin into ethereum rather than exiting crypto. September's flows remain net positive despite the weekly reversal.

03

CLARITY Act vote and Fed rate decision make a heavy week for crypto

The US Senate votes Tuesday at 2:15 pm ET on advancing the CLARITY Act, a 635-page bill to give blockchain and digital asset markets a clear legal framework. Senator Cynthia Lummis called the revised text the "last, best and final offer."

On Wednesday, the Federal Reserve is widely expected to raise rates by 25 basis points to a range of 3.75%-4%. CME FedWatch data puts the odds of a hold at just 13.3%, down from above 40% a week ago.

Traders are already cutting risk: open interest in BTC terms fell 13.5% in the week through Sept. 11 to 278,151 BTC, according to Santiment. Polymarket gives the bill a 34% chance of being signed into law in 2026.

Both verdicts arrive within 48 hours, with oil above $100 per barrel keeping inflation pressure alive. Expect choppy trading across the cryptocurrency market this week.

Why it matters

A failed vote plus a rate hike is the worst combination for bitcoin this month. A clean pass could offset the Fed shock.
