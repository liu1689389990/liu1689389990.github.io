Sep 12, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin whipsaws near $78K as hot core CPI pushes the Fed

Bitcoin whipsaws near $78K as hot core CPI pushes the Fed toward a rate hike — here is what moved crypto markets overnight.

01

US Core CPI Runs Hotter Than Forecast, Setting Up a Possible Fed Rate Hike

August core CPI rose 0.3% month-over-month, above expectations — and this print carries extra weight. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh signaled two weeks ago the central bank may have to act if inflation does not slow, and markets now price a rate hike as near-certain. Higher rates make bonds and cash more attractive versus risk assets like Bitcoin, which briefly dipped below $77,000 before stabilizing. The takeaway: the macro calendar, not crypto-native flows, is driving prices right now, and the next Fed decision is the single biggest catalyst on the board.

02

Bitcoin ETF Outflows Accelerate — $449M Pulled in Three Days

US spot Bitcoin ETFs bled $449 million over three sessions, with ARK 21Shares alone accounting for $164 million of Thursday's withdrawals. Ethereum and Solana funds also recorded net outflows, so this is a broad de-risking move, not a single-fund event. ETF flows are the clearest window into institutional conviction, and three straight days of exits suggest big money is sidelining ahead of the Fed decision rather than buying the dip. If withdrawals continue after the rate call, expect further downside pressure on spot prices; a dovish surprise could flip this trend quickly.

03

Blockstream Refuses Ransom Over $47M in Stolen Bitcoin From Liquid Hack: "It Is Theft"

Blockstream, the company behind Bitcoin's Liquid sidechain, says it will not pay a ransom for the return of 598.5 BTC (~$47M) still held by attackers from the $320M Liquid exploit. The firm says it will hand the case to law enforcement if the funds are not returned, and the network itself has already resumed block production. This is a litmus test for crypto's extortion era: paying ransoms rewards attackers and invites copycats, while refusing risks watching the coins disappear into mixers. Traders holding Liquid-issued assets should watch for follow-up disclosures on frozen or moved funds.
