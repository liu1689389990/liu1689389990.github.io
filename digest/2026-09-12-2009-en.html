Sep 12, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin price today, Sept. 12: BTC settled near $77,400 af

Bitcoin price today, Sept. 12: BTC settled near $77,400 after fading a spike to $79,837, and Fed rate hike odds jumped to 86%. Here's the crypto news that moved markets in the US session.

01

Bitcoin's golden cross fades as Fed rate hike odds jump to 86%

Bitcoin's 50-day moving average briefly crossed above its 200-day average on Friday, a bullish pattern traders call a golden cross, before slipping back under as BTC retreated to $77,438 from a session high near $79,837.

The reversal tracked hotter inflation data. US core CPI rose 0.3% last month from the previous month, above the 0.2% economists expected, and CME FedWatch data showed odds of a quarter-point Fed rate hike at next week's meeting jumped from 69% to 86.5% within hours.

Bitcoin opened the day at $76,529, touched a low of $76,040 and still closed up 1.2%. Ethereum traded around $2,535, up about 3%, and altcoins including Solana and XRP also gained.

Why it matters

A daily golden cross has historically preceded Bitcoin's bigger rallies, but it is a lagging signal built from past prices. The Fed decision next week matters more than any chart pattern right now.

02

Hyperliquid leads DeFi perp trading, but regulation is its biggest risk

Hyperliquid, a layer-1 blockchain best known for its decentralized perpetual futures exchange, logged about $223 billion in trading volume over the past 30 days, more than any rival, according to DeFiLlama.

Crypto Banter founder Ran Neuner told Cointelegraph's Chain Reaction podcast that regulators have started writing rules for centralized crypto exchanges and that decentralized platforms could be next. "You can't copy a network," he said, arguing Hyperliquid's liquidity and user base protect it from copycats.

The HYPE token traded around $82 on Friday, up more than 220% this year, with a market value near $18.2 billion, according to CoinGecko data. President Trump said in August that CFTC Chair Michael Selig was working to bring Hyperliquid into the US in a "fully compliant and legal fashion."

Why it matters

Washington's posture will decide whether the largest DeFi venue can serve US traders openly. Whatever rulebook emerges will apply to every decentralized exchange after it.

03

Bitcoin Suisse moves up to half of its Swiss jobs abroad

Bitcoin Suisse, the crypto services firm founded in Zug in 2013, plans to move up to 60 of its 120 Swiss positions to lower-cost hubs in Bratislava and Vietnam, Swiss financial outlet Finews reported on Friday.

CEO Andrej Majcen said cost drove the decision and that the restructuring is not a response to weakness in crypto markets. The company is expanding into wealth and asset management and has secured licenses in Liechtenstein and Bermuda this year, plus full regulatory approval in Abu Dhabi in July.

Why it matters

One of Europe's oldest crypto companies is relocating back-office work to wherever it runs cheapest. That is a sign the industry is managing costs like a mature finance business.
