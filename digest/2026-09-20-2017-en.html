Sep 20, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin price today: BTC trades near $80,400 on Sept. 20 a

Bitcoin price today: BTC trades near $80,400 on Sept. 20 after surviving a week of Washington shocks that wiped out $571 million in bullish bets. Ethereum fell 2.4% to $2,577. Here's the crypto news moving markets tonight.

01

Bitcoin holds $80,000 as analysts split after a brutal week

Bitcoin trades around $80,400 on Sunday, down about 1% over 24 hours, after shrugging off the Senate's failure to pass the CLARITY Act. In the first 24 hours after the vote, traders holding bullish futures saw $571 million liquidated, and Coinbase and Circle shares slid 10%.

Analysts now expect range-bound trading. A move above $80,000 could open the way to $81,400, while a fall below $75,000 would put the recovery in question, with $77,950 and $79,300 the first levels to clear.

Why it matters

Bitcoin's stability through the vote suggests large investors are staying on the sidelines rather than selling. The next direction likely comes from macro data, not Washington headlines.

02

Coinbase, Robinhood and Circle could win from the SEC's tokenized-stock push

The SEC's new five-year innovation exemption lets tokenized US stocks trade through automated market makers on public blockchains. Analysts at Goldman Sachs and Citizens named Coinbase, Robinhood and Circle as early beneficiaries.

To qualify, tokens must preserve shareholder rights such as dividends and voting. Goldman said Coinbase's tokenized-equity product already meets many requirements, while Robinhood must rebuild its offshore stock tokens to comply. Circle could gain as more settlement and collateral flows through USDC.

Why it matters

Trading caps and issuer opt-outs shield traditional exchanges for now, but this is the first concrete path for US equities onto Ethereum-based DeFi rails.

03

Grayscale splits its Zcash ETF 3-for-1 after a $233M first month

Grayscale filed a 3-for-1 forward split for its Zcash ETF (ZCSH), which has pulled in more than $233 million since its Aug. 25 launch and holds roughly $890 million in net assets.

The privacy altcoin Zcash rose more than 2,800% over the past year and hit an intraday record near $1,521 on Friday. At the Sept. 28 close, each holder gets two extra shares per share owned, cutting the per-share price to a third without changing total value. Zcash traded near $1,450 on Sunday, down 6% on the day.

This week: watch bitcoin's $80,000 level, follow-through on the SEC tokenization exemption and the Sept. 28 Zcash split.

Why it matters

Cheaper shares lower the entry barrier for small investors, a sign Wall Street is packaging niche altcoins for retail demand.
