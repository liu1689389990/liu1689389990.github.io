Sep 18, 2026 Crypto Close · Bitcoin trades above $77,000 as the Bank of Japan hikes ra

Bitcoin trades above $77,000 as the Bank of Japan hikes rates to a 31-year high, a day after the Fed rate hike. Here's what moved crypto markets today.

01

Bank of Japan raises rates by 25 basis points to a 31-year high

The Bank of Japan raised its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points on Friday to 1.25%. That is the highest level in 31 years, as it battles sticky inflation and a weak yen.

The move marks the central bank's second hike in three months. Bitcoin jumped to $77,400 from an overnight low of $76,200, according to CoinDesk data. The yen weakened to 156.70 per dollar.

Why it matters

Japan's rate stays far below U.S. levels, so the yen-funded carry trade that buys crypto keeps running. A sudden unwind of that trade could hit risk assets, but the risk is low for now.

02

Ether and XRP ETF outflows hit a third day as bitcoin climbs

U.S. spot ether ETFs posted about $39 million of net outflows on Thursday, a third straight day of withdrawals, after $224 million left on Wednesday and $141 million left on Tuesday, according to SoSoValue.

XRP funds lost about $5 million. Bitcoin ETFs took in about $159 million, and the single U.S. Zcash fund added nearly $47 million, its best day in a month that has brought it more than $230 million.

Over 30 days the ether funds remain more than $1.5 billion ahead and bitcoin nearly $2.5 billion. Major altcoins including Zcash and Solana rose alongside bitcoin.

Why it matters

The split between ether outflows and bitcoin inflows suggests investors are trimming ethereum exposure while keeping bitcoin bets — and that's crypto news worth watching ahead of the next Fed decision.

#BitcoinETF#Ethereum
03

Zcash targets November for NU7 mainnet upgrade with 25-second blocks

Zcash developers are targeting November 5 for the NU7 mainnet activation after reaching unanimous agreement on the upgrade's contents and timeline.

The upgrade cuts Zcash's target block spacing from 75 seconds to 25 seconds and keeps the coin's halving schedule intact, with supply reintroduction beginning in February 2031. The upgrade is part of a push to make the Zcash blockchain more responsive for payments.

Keep an eye on the ether fund flow trend and the October 6 Zcash testnet as the next catalysts. Bitcoin is holding above $77,000 for now.

Why it matters

Faster blocks could make Zcash more usable for payments, and holders backed the change by 98.9% in a recent vote.
