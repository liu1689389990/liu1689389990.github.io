Bitcoin price today, Sept. 12: BTC holds around $77,000 and ether trades near $2,510 as Asian hours set the tone. Today's crypto news covers Robinhood's August volume, India's tokenized bonds and Metaplanet's Hong Kong plan.

01

Robinhood's crypto trading volume jumps 61% in August

Robinhood's crypto volume rose 61% from July to $17.5 billion in August, according to operating data the company released Thursday. The rebound follows a slow July at $10.9 billion.

Even with the jump, the total came in 38% below the $28.1 billion Robinhood processed in August 2025. Event contracts, its prediction-market bets, traded 4.7 billion times last month.

Why it matters

Retail crypto trading on a major US broker is recovering ahead of the Fed's Sept. 16 rate decision. The 38% year-over-year gap shows activity is still far below its 2025 peak.

02

India launches tokenized bond pilot with $107 million issued

India's securities regulator and central bank launched a tokenized corporate bond pilot, with three companies issuing a combined 10.25 billion rupees, about $107 million, the Securities and Exchange Board of India said Thursday.

State lender REC raised 5 billion rupees from 18 investors, engineering firm Larsen & Toubro added another 5 billion rupees and IIFL issued 250 million rupees. Settlement runs on the Reserve Bank of India's wholesale digital currency.

SEBI said issuers now get funded on bidding day instead of two to three days later, and later phases will add resale trading and retail access.

Why it matters

A large emerging market is putting bonds on blockchain rails inside its regulated system. SEBI says India is the first country to combine token issuance, depository records and CBDC settlement, a template other Asian regulators could follow.

03

Metaplanet cuts its executive stock pool by 41% and plans a Hong Kong trading arm

Metaplanet will remove 131.3 million potential shares from its Series 10 stock acquisition rights, cutting the pool from 319.464 million to 188.19 million shares after shareholder backlash over dilution, CEO Simon Gerovich announced Friday.

The change wipes out more than $220 million in warrant value and lifts bitcoin per fully diluted share by about 8.8%, Gerovich said.

The Japanese bitcoin treasury firm also plans a $1 million Hong Kong subsidiary this month to trade bitcoin, equities and credit products during Asian hours. Shares fell 3.8% on Friday, extending a five-day slide to 15%.

Why it matters

Asia's biggest corporate bitcoin holder is giving ground to shareholders while expanding deeper into the region.
